## Mission and Requirement

The aerobot descends through Venus's atmosphere for 48 hours. Its electronics and science instruments have to stay near 300 K while the outside temperature ranges from 225.8 K to 344.7 K.

## Thermal Modeling

Steady-state energy balances were built for the hot and cold extremes, including solar flux, albedo, radiation, and convection on every face.

## Design

A hybrid **passive–active** system:

- **Multilayer insulation (MLI)** on five faces
- **Z93 white-coated radiator** on the sixth face to reject heat in the hot case
- **Loop heat pipe** to move heat from the electronics to the radiator
- **Polyimide heater** to hold temperature in the cold case

Trade studies balanced mass, power, cost, and performance. The design was reworked after a late-stage change to the mission scope.

## Systems Engineering

- Coordinated thermal requirements with the mechanical, power, CDH, and payload subsystems
- Completed NASA L'SPACE training in requirements, risk, project management, heat transfer, and Siemens NX
- Took the design through the MCR, SRR, MDR, and PDR mission reviews

## Results

The thermal subsystem kept the payload at 300 K in both extreme cases while staying within its mass, power, and budget allocations. It was documented in the team's 191-page Preliminary Design Review.
