## Analog & Timing

- **RC timer + op-amp comparator** for delayed LED activation (~10 s)
- **Op-amp difference amplifier** implementing V<sub>out</sub> = V<sub>1</sub> − V<sub>2</sub> on dual supply rails
- **555 one-shot timer** producing a repeatable ~9–10 s pulse

## Digital Logic

- **Full adder** from XOR, AND, and OR gates, verified for all 8 input combinations

## Microcontroller I/O

- Digital I/O with switch-controlled LED logic
- Potentiometer → ADC → PWM brightness control
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance measurement shown on an LCD

## Actuators

- DC motor speed and direction control with PWM
- Positional and continuous-rotation servo control, later used for motion sequencing in [GOAL!](/projects/goal-penalty-shootout/)
