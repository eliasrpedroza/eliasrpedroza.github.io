Hi, I'm Elias.

I'm a robotics engineer with a mechanical engineering foundation, currently pursuing an M.S. in Robotics at Northwestern University. I graduated cum laude in Mechanical Engineering from Santa Clara University.

My work sits between hardware and software: designing mechanisms, building and debugging electromechanical systems, and increasingly, the controls and manipulation that make them useful. Outside of projects, I've served as Community Outreach Director for SHPE and Secretary of ASME, and I've mentored students in SCU's Summer Engineering Seminar.
